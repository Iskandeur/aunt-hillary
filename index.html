    How to read this
    • Colours: sand, water, plant, wall. Bright tinted cells are living bodies; white ones are loose life that belongs to nobody.
    • A body is 24 or more touching life cells. It gets a number (#12). It can split when it grows past 150 cells, or be absorbed when it touches a bigger one.
    • A mind: only the 8 biggest bodies think. A thinking mind looks at its surroundings, then rewrites the rules of its own cells (eat plants, grow, turn water into plants…) and may say a word to the bodies touching it.
    • Watch: a pulsing outline means that body is thinking right now; its thought then appears in a bubble. Every change is told in plain words in What's happening. Click a line to select that body.
    • Why it is slow: Slow shows one thought at a time, about every 8 s. Only thoughts from a real model cost money; the physics is free. Use Pause / Normal / Fast to change pace.
